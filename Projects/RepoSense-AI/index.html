Local-first feedback

Your corrections are stored only in browser storage and the model is immediately retrained.

Prototype limitations: the small starter dataset is synthetic and English-only. Relative fit is not a calibrated probability. Priority is rule-based. Always review outcomes, especially for incidents and security reports.
